# Brief

The idea that was typed into the wizard, word for word:

> Explain why octopuses have three hearts and blue blood, and how this strange anatomy helps them survive in cold, deep water. The video is for curious viewers of a faceless science channel: friendly, clear and visual, with one surprising fact per scene and a short takeaway at the end.

Why it works as a brief (see [chapter 2](../../guide/02-writing-for-the-voice.md)):

- **It names the subject and the facts**: three hearts, blue blood, survival in cold deep water. The planner kept that order.
- **It names the audience and the tone**: curious viewers of a faceless science channel, friendly and clear.
- **It gives the writer a rule per scene** ("one surprising fact per scene") and **says how to end** ("a short takeaway"). Both shaped the script: every middle scene carries one surprising fact, and the last scene is a one-sentence takeaway.

Settings:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Landscape, 16:9 (YouTube) |
| Target length | just over a minute |
| Language | English |
| Writing style | Clear explainer |
| Captions | Karaoke preset (short phrases, a pill behind the current word) |
| Background theme | Blue |
