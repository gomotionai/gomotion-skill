# Brief

The one-sentence idea that was sent to `create --idea`, word for word:

> Why the sky is blue, explained in one minute: sunlight is made of every colour, air molecules scatter short blue wavelengths far more than red ones (Rayleigh scattering), so blue light reaches our eyes from every direction; at sunset the light crosses more air, the blue is scattered away and reds remain. End with why Mars has a butterscotch sky.

Why it works as a brief (see [references/scriptwriting.md](../../references/scriptwriting.md)):

- **It names the hook** ("explained in one minute") and the payoff ("end with why Mars has a butterscotch sky"), so the planner knows how to open and how to close.
- **It lists the facts to include**, in the order they should appear. The planner wrote five scenes that follow that order.
- **It states the audience and the tone** implicitly: a curious general audience, one idea at a time, no formulas.

Settings used:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `--duration` | 60 (the planner writes to this length) |
| `--ratio` | 16:9 |
| `--language` | en (170 words per minute at the default voice) |
| voice, theme, captions | defaults (`theme: blue`, `captionPresetId: bold-stroke`) |
